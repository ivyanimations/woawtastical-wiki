Reggie's Cave

Back →

Reggie's Cave is the place Reggie lives when he isn't at the outpost. It is comprised of the destroyed remains of the ship he came in as well as stuff he has gathered on his travels. It has 4 main rooms: Reggie's sleeping area, Gumpus and Runa's area, the multipurpose space, and the entrance area.

Trivia

  • The used cave is around the size of a small apartment, as Reggie has yet to explore deeper.
  • It gets power from solar panels he has placed outside the cave.
  • The space is very warm due to it being well insulated and the planets temperature.
  • Despite not being very well guarded, the cave is very well hidden, leading to almost nobody finding it by accident.
  • It tends to be very messy, as Reggie and sometimes Gumpus and Runa are the only ones to stay there or know its location.
