Reggie is a slightly stupid, outgoing, and energetic alien. His only true goal in life is to have fun and cause chaos, something he is very good at. When his home planet was destroyed, he crash landed on a planet, which he dubbed Nightrock (how clever). Eventually, he ran into Gumpus, a survivor of another crash on the planet, whom he befriends almost instantly.

Trivia

  • He really enjoys salty foods, with his favorite being pretzels.
    • His second favorite food is saltine crackers.
  • Despite the death of his species, he retains a positive outlook on life. He believes that he should enjoy life as much as possible because it might one day all be over without his control.
  • He is neurodivergent.
  • His species lives almost double the length of the average human.
  • He enjoys tinkering and making little machines, although they usually don't work very well.
