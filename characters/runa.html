Runa

Back →

Runa is an inverted clone of Gumpus created in The Cave of Dreams. She tends to be very shy and anxious. After her creation, she is brought home by Gumpus and later adopted by Jane, becoming Gumpus's sister. She frequently joins Gumpus and Reggie on their adventures. She has abilities granted by the cave however, she has not learned them yet.

Trivia

  • In her free time, she enjoys painting rocks.
  • Her favorite color is sunrise.
  • She dislikes overly sweet foods, including most candies.
    • Because of this, her top 2 favorite foods are moss and kale.
  • She has most of the skills and wordly knowledge Gumpus had when she was created however, she does not share any of Gumpus's specific memories.
  • Despite never having tasted one, she despises the concept of burgers, sausages, and meat of that variety.
    • To the surprise of nobody, she is vegetarian.
